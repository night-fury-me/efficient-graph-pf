\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%% ===================================================================

Each of three threads supplies at most one of \AEGIS's diagnostics. \textbf{Structural attacks} (Nettack, Mettack, GR-BCD/PR-BCD and variants~\cite{zugner2018adversarial,zugner2019adversarial,geisler2021robustness,wu2019adversarial,xu2019topology}) optimise gradients to flip predictions but yield no analytic direction and no per-node radius; sober-look studies~\cite{mujkanovic2022defenses,gosch2023adversarial} flag the evaluation pitfalls we adopt (\cref{app:ablations}).

\textbf{Certified robustness and defense.} Convex relaxations~\cite{zugner2019certifiable}, smoothing~\cite{bojchevski2020efficient,bojchevski2019certifiable,schuchardt2023localized}, collective~\cite{schuchardt2021collective} and majority-vote~\cite{li2025agnncert} certificates, and weight-norm bounds~\cite{abbahaddou2024bounding} certify robustness but do not name vulnerability-driving edges; empirical defenses~\cite{zhu2019robust,jin2020graph,zhang2020gnnguard} harden models without surfacing per-edge sensitivity. Graph conformal prediction~\cite{zargarbashi2023conformal} certifies a \emph{fixed} graph, whereas \AEGIS-Conformal covers the adversarial $\varepsilon$-ball via the $S_c$ score-shift bound (\cref{sec:conformal}).

\textbf{Implicit networks and influence functions.} DEQs, IGNN, monotone equilibria, and well-posedness analyses~\cite{bai2019deep,gu2020implicit,winston2020monotone,el2021implicit} address \emph{input} sensitivity $\partial \zstar/\partial x$; \AEGIS targets \emph{structural} sensitivity $\partial Z/\partial A$, applying the Koh--Liang influence lineage~\cite{koh2017understanding} (validated against deep-net influence fragility~\cite{basu2021influence} by our finite-difference checks) and implicit differentiation~\cite{lorraine2020optimizing,gould2021deep} to a structural perturbation via $P_c$ rather than to a loss. GNN explainers~\cite{ying2019gnnexplainer} rank edges for \emph{fidelity}, not vulnerability; matrix perturbation theory~\cite{stewart1990matrix,trefethen2005spectra} supplies the pseudospectral toolkit; and curvature/over-squashing~\cite{topping2022understanding,digiovanni2023oversquashing} and stability bounds~\cite{gama2020stability,kenlay2021stability} tie edge structure to sensitivity only in aggregate, whereas \AEGIS localises it per edge and node.
