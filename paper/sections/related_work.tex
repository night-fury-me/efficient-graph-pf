\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%% ===================================================================

We position \AEGIS against four threads; each supplies at most one of the three outputs we unify under a single matrix-free pass.

\textbf{Structural attacks.} Nettack~\cite{zugner2018adversarial}, Mettack~\cite{zugner2019adversarial}, GR-BCD/PR-BCD~\cite{geisler2021robustness}, and variants~\cite{wu2019adversarial} optimise surrogate gradients to flip predictions but provide no closed-form globally maximally sensitive direction. Sober-look studies~\cite{mujkanovic2022defenses,gosch2023adversarial} flag evaluation pitfalls; the defence-informed-masking ablation (\cref{sec:defense_ablation}) uses paired Wilcoxon against random masking for this reason.

\textbf{Certified robustness and defense.} Convex relaxations~\cite{zugner2019certifiable}, smoothing~\cite{bojchevski2020efficient,bojchevski2019certifiable,schuchardt2023localized}, collective certificates~\cite{schuchardt2021collective}, and IBP~\cite{li2025agnncert} certify per-node robustness but do not identify the vulnerability-driving edges. Empirical defenses~\cite{zhu2019robust,jin2020graph,zhang2020gnnguard} harden models without surfacing a vulnerability map. $r_v$ is a first-order threshold, not a sound certificate (\cref{rem:certificates}); the two are complementary.

\textbf{Implicit networks and influence functions.} DEQs~\cite{bai2019deep}, IGNN~\cite{gu2020implicit}, and well-posedness analyses~\cite{el2021implicit} address input sensitivity $\partial \zstar/\partial x$; \AEGIS targets structural sensitivity $\partial Z/\partial A$ via the resolvent $(I{-}J_z)^{-1}J_A$ (implicit; \cref{thm:phase_transition}) and the unrolled Jacobian $S_K$ (explicit; \cref{prop:explicit}). The closed-form derivative descends from influence-function implicit differentiation~\cite{lorraine2020optimizing}, extended to adjacency under a constrained projection; GNN explainers~\cite{ying2019gnnexplainer} target prediction fidelity rather than adversarial vulnerability, and we draw on classical matrix perturbation theory~\cite{stewart1990matrix,trefethen2005spectra}.
