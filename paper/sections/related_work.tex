\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%% ===================================================================

We position \AEGIS against three threads; each supplies at most one of the three diagnostics \AEGIS produces from one $S_c$ object via a single matrix-free pipeline.

\textbf{Structural attacks.} Nettack~\cite{zugner2018adversarial}, Mettack~\cite{zugner2019adversarial}, GR-BCD/PR-BCD~\cite{geisler2021robustness} and variants~\cite{wu2019adversarial} optimise surrogate gradients to flip predictions but provide no analytic maximally sensitive first-order direction and no per-node radius. Sober-look studies~\cite{mujkanovic2022defenses,gosch2023adversarial} flag evaluation pitfalls; we follow their adaptive-attack discipline in \cref{sec:defense_ablation} (paired Wilcoxon against random masking; an adaptive-attacker column in defense ablation).

\textbf{Certified robustness and defense.} Convex relaxations~\cite{zugner2019certifiable}, smoothing~\cite{bojchevski2020efficient,bojchevski2019certifiable,schuchardt2023localized}, collective certificates~\cite{schuchardt2021collective}, and IBP~\cite{li2025agnncert} certify per-node robustness but do not identify vulnerability-driving edges. Empirical defenses~\cite{zhu2019robust,jin2020graph,zhang2020gnnguard} harden models without surfacing per-edge sensitivity. $r_v$ is a first-order threshold, not a sound certificate (\cref{rem:certificates}); the methods are complementary under the prescribed decision rule of \cref{tab:baselines}.

\textbf{Implicit networks and influence functions.} DEQs~\cite{bai2019deep}, IGNN~\cite{gu2020implicit}, monotone equilibria~\cite{winston2020monotone}, and well-posedness analyses~\cite{el2021implicit} address \emph{input} sensitivity $\partial \zstar/\partial x$; \AEGIS targets \emph{structural} sensitivity $\partial Z/\partial A$ via the resolvent $(I{-}J_z)^{-1}J_A$ and the unrolled Jacobian $S_K$. The construction descends from the Koh--Liang influence-function lineage~\cite{koh2017understanding} and implicit-differentiation hyperparameter optimisation~\cite{lorraine2020optimizing,gould2021deep}, but those apply IFT to loss perturbations; \AEGIS applies it to a \emph{structural-parameter} perturbation, projecting onto the edge subspace via $P_c$ for the SVD-optimal direction in one matrix-free pass. GNN explainers~\cite{ying2019gnnexplainer} produce per-edge importance, the closest analogue to our per-edge ranking, but for prediction \emph{fidelity} (which subgraph rationalises an output) rather than adversarial vulnerability; classical matrix perturbation theory~\cite{stewart1990matrix,trefethen2005spectra} supplies the pseudospectral toolkit.
