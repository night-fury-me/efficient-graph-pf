\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
%% ===================================================================

Graph neural networks are now deployed in domains where structural errors carry tangible consequences: fraudulent accounts evade detection via perturbed transaction edges~\cite{zugner2018adversarial}, drug-interaction models misfire under perturbed molecular graphs~\cite{dai2018adversarial}, and power grids can miss critical contingencies when their graph representations are structurally fragile~\cite{nakiganda2023graph}. A practitioner deploying a GNN faces a question current tools leave unanswered: \emph{which edges, if perturbed, would cause predictions to fail, and by how much?}

Structural attacks~\cite{zugner2018adversarial,zugner2019adversarial,geisler2021robustness,wu2019adversarial} give per-edge surrogate gradients but no continuous direction or per-node budgets; smoothing~\cite{bojchevski2020efficient,bojchevski2019certifiable,schuchardt2023localized,schuchardt2021collective} and IBP-style certifiers~\cite{li2025agnncert,zugner2019certifiable} give per-node certificates but no edge rankings or direction; robust-architecture defenses~\cite{zhu2019robust,zhang2020gnnguard,jin2020graph} harden models without surfacing per-edge vulnerability; sober-look critiques~\cite{mujkanovic2022defenses,gosch2023adversarial} flag evaluation pitfalls. \AEGIS (Adversarial Evaluation of Graph Integrity via Sensitivity) produces all three from one analytical object, $S_c$ (\cref{sec:framework})---``one query'' meaning a single matrix-free $S_c$ construction (one randomized SVD over the equilibrium resolvent), not three separate analyses. The closed-form regime characterisation (\cref{thm:phase_transition}) is established for contractive implicit GNNs and $S_c$ extends to any GNN with continuous edge-weight-modulated message passing (\cref{sec:explicit_extension}); the threat model targets edge-deletion and weight-perturbation (\cref{sec:background}).

\textbf{Contributions.} \textbf{(1) Constrained sensitivity operator.} $S_c$ specialises equilibrium IFT sensitivity~\cite{koh2017understanding,gould2021deep} to \emph{structural} edge perturbations via the projection $P_c$; one matrix-free query reads the SVD-optimal direction, per-edge rankings, and per-node radii (dense-matching $\sigma_1$ to $0.03\%$ at $N{=}200$; scaling to $N{=}7{,}650$)---no prior object yields all three at once. \textbf{(2) Phase-transition theory.} A worst-case three-regime characterisation with closed-form critical budget $\ecrit{=}(1{-}\kappa)/\norm{W}_2$ for the spectral-norm-constrained IGNN subclass, which trained IGNNs satisfy with $2$--$4\times$ margin across our $\kappa_{\max}$ sweep, giving practitioners a provable structural-perturbation safety threshold ($\sim 6\%$ accuracy cost; \cref{thm:phase_transition,sec:phase_transition}). \textbf{(3) Empirical evaluation.} 9 datasets, 7 architectures, 4 domains (330 runs): four-quadrant attack comparison, head-to-head vs.\ GR-BCD/PR-BCD~\cite{geisler2021robustness}, AGNNCert~\cite{li2025agnncert}, and LODF (\cref{tab:baselines}), continuous-to-discrete transfer (\cref{fig:tau_heatmap}), and a power-grid N-1 case study (\cref{sec:power_flow}).
