\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ===================================================================

From one matrix-free object, $S_c$, \AEGIS \emph{audits}, \emph{certifies}, and \emph{defends} any GNN with continuous edge-weight message passing, adding the safety boundary of \cref{thm:phase_transition} for contractive implicit models. Three once-separate tools become readings of a single operator, coupled by construction.

\textbf{Limitations.} The radii $r_v$ are first-order thresholds, and \AEGIS-Conformal runs densely at $N{=}200$. Standard GAT and binary-mask architectures fall outside the framework (\cref{sec:explicit_extension}), the $\ecrit$ track costs ${\sim}6\%$ accuracy, and insertion attacks are scoped out (\cref{sec:background}). \AEGIS also audits vulnerability, not physics: a contractive surrogate cannot model voltage collapse, so it complements rather than replaces power-flow contingency screening~\cite{donon2019graph,nakiganda2023graph,varbella2024contingency}.

\textbf{Disclosure.} We propose a 90-day coordinated-notification window: the diagnostic path ($r_v$, $v_{ij}$) releases unconditionally, while attack-direction reconstruction (\cref{alg:aegis}) is gated behind ethics review, since it alone yields the damaging $\delta A^*$ (\cref{prop:attack}).
