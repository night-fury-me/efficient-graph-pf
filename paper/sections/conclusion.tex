\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ===================================================================

From one matrix-free object, $S_c$, \AEGIS unifies auditing, certification, and defense as readings of a single operator, coupled by construction and extending to any GNN with continuous edge-weight message passing; a closed-form safety boundary (\cref{thm:phase_transition}) covers contractive implicit models.

\textbf{Limitations.} The radii $r_v$ are first-order thresholds, and \AEGIS-Conformal runs densely at $N{=}200$. Standard GAT and binary-mask architectures fall outside the framework (\cref{sec:explicit_extension}), and insertion attacks are scoped out (\cref{sec:background}). The $\ecrit$ track costs ${\sim}6\%$ accuracy. \AEGIS audits vulnerability, not physics: a contractive surrogate cannot model voltage collapse, so it complements rather than replaces power-flow contingency screening~\cite{donon2019graph,nakiganda2023graph,varbella2024contingency}.

\textbf{Disclosure.} We propose a 90-day coordinated-notification window: the diagnostic path ($r_v$, $v_{ij}$) releases unconditionally, while attack-direction reconstruction (\cref{alg:aegis}) is gated behind ethics review, since it alone yields the damaging $\delta A^*$ (\cref{prop:attack}).
