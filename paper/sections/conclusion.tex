\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ===================================================================

AEGIS provides a principled pre-deployment diagnostic for GNNs on safety-critical graphs: from a single $S_c$ computation, practitioners obtain the optimal attack direction, per-edge vulnerability rankings, and per-node tolerance budgets. Predictions are tight across 7 architectures and 9 datasets (tightness $1.00 \pm 0.01$ at $\varepsilon = 0.01$), and the vulnerability spectrum transfers to power grid contingency analysis (P@10 = 0.66--0.81). The per-edge ranking directly informs defense design (\cref{sec:defense_ablation}).

\textbf{Limitations.} (1)~First-order sensitivity radii are locally tight but not global certificates without second-order bounds. (2)~Continuous edge-weight perturbations; discrete insertions/deletions are outside the formal guarantee. (3)~Dense Jacobian limits subgraph size to $N \approx 300$. (4)~Standard GAT requires edge-weighted modification for continuous sensitivity. (5)~Power flow $\tau = 0.37$--$0.67$ requires verification for operational use~\cite{wood2014power}.

\textbf{Future work.} Sparse solvers for larger subgraphs; second-order bounds for formal certificates; discrete perturbation models; integration with defense mechanisms~\cite{jin2020graph,zhang2020gnnguard} for vulnerability-aware training.
