\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ===================================================================

\AEGIS produces three adversarial diagnostics (per-edge rankings, the SVD-optimal attack direction, per-node first-order sensitivity radii) from one analytical object, the constrained sensitivity matrix $S_c$, computed matrix-free for any GNN with continuous edge-weight-modulated message passing, with a regime characterisation (\cref{thm:phase_transition}) that holds as a sufficient safety boundary for contractive implicit models.

\textbf{Limitations.} (i) Radii $r_v$ are first-order thresholds, not probabilistic certificates (\cref{rem:certificates}); standard GAT and binary-mask architectures fall outside the framework (\cref{sec:explicit_extension}). (ii) Full-graph matrix-free analysis is the recommended graph-level path: AtkAdv amplifies ($3.3{\times}/9.8{\times}$ on Cora/Citeseer) and the edge-weighted ranking $A_{ij}v_{ij}$ achieves $\tau\!=\!\mathbf{+0.996}$ on Amazon Photo ($N{=}7{,}650$; \cref{sec:explicit_extension}). (iii) Opting into the formal $\ecrit$ track accepts a ${\sim}6\%$ accuracy cost, on par with certified-robustness trade-offs. (iv) Operator-grade power-flow deployment (admittance-weighted edges, broader load envelopes) is a natural follow-up. (v) Insertion attacks (Nettack-class) are deliberately scoped out; the $S_c$ construction extends once the edge basis is enlarged to $\bar E \supseteq E$ (\cref{sec:background}).

\textbf{Disclosure protocol.} (i)~A 90-day coordinated-notification window before public release, addressed to the maintainers of OGB graph-robustness benchmarks and to the GR-BCD / Mettack / AGNNCert authors. (ii)~Attack-direction code (Algorithm~\ref{alg:aegis} steps 8--9) gated behind institutional-affiliation review mediated by an institutional research-ethics office (named in the camera-ready). (iii)~The diagnostic-only path ($r_v$ and $v_{ij}$ scores; no SVD reconstruction) released unconditionally, since it cannot directly synthesise a perturbation. Power-grid artefacts use only public IEEE benchmarks~\cite{ieee2024testcases}.
