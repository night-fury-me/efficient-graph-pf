\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ===================================================================

From one matrix-free object, $S_c$, \AEGIS \emph{audits}, \emph{certifies}, and \emph{defends} any GNN with continuous edge-weight message passing, adding for contractive implicit models the safety boundary of \cref{thm:phase_transition}.

\textbf{Limitations.} The radii $r_v$ are first-order thresholds, and \AEGIS-Conformal runs densely at $N{=}200$ (a matrix-free pass is future work). Standard GAT and binary-mask architectures fall outside the framework (\cref{sec:explicit_extension}), the $\ecrit$ track costs ${\sim}6\%$ accuracy, and insertion attacks are scoped out (\cref{sec:background}). Finally, \AEGIS audits model vulnerability, not physics: a contractive surrogate cannot represent voltage collapse, complementing not replacing power-flow contingency screening~\cite{donon2019graph,nakiganda2023graph,varbella2024contingency}.

\textbf{Disclosure.} We propose a 90-day coordinated-notification window: the diagnostic path ($r_v$, $v_{ij}$) releases unconditionally, while attack-direction reconstruction (\cref{alg:aegis}) is gated behind ethics review---only it yields the damaging $\delta A^*$ (\cref{prop:attack}).
