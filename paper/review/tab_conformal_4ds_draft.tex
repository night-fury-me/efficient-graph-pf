% --- prose sentence preceding the table (replaces the current one) ---
\Cref{tab:conformal} reports coverage over the 10 seeds for four datasets. The \emph{gate}---coverage under the worst-case \AEGIS attack at magnitude $\varepsilon$ (reconverging the equilibrium after the $v_1$ perturbation)---holds at the nominal $1{-}\alpha{=}0.90$ at $\varepsilon{=}0.01$ on every dataset and is conservative ($0.94$--$1.00$) at $\varepsilon{=}0.05$, with \emph{zero} equilibrium divergence across all gate nodes. Sets are ${\sim}1.0$--$1.5$ labels on the citation graphs (6--7 classes) and ${\sim}3.5$ on WikiCS (10 classes), non-vacuous in every case and tightest exactly where the deterministic radius certifies few nodes; the certificate uses zero Monte-Carlo samples and via $S_K$ applies to any GNN. Exchangeability on a single transductive graph is a stated condition (the empirical gate is the evidence); we form $S_c$ densely at $N{=}200$, a matrix-free conformal pass being future work.
