## The cross-cutting reframe (the umbrella)

THE UMBRELLA: 'AEGIS is the sensitivity GEOMETRY of equilibrium graph models, carrying a certified attack-defense DUALITY from a single operator.' Reframe the whole paper around one claim: the constrained sensitivity matrix S_c = (I-J_z)^{-1}J_A P_c is not a Jacobian diagnostic but the canonical first-order GEOMETRY of the equilibrium prediction map restricted to the edge polytope, and from this ONE object - computed once, matrix-free - flow BOTH the sharpest attack and a sound defense against it, the two sides bound by the SAME resolvent (I-J_z)^{-1} and the SAME critical budget eps_crit. Concretely, S_c is simultaneously (i) a Riemannian PULLBACK FISHER METRIC whose top eigenvector is the KL-optimal attack and whose spectrum sets a Cramer-Rao DETECTABILITY floor (AEGIS-Fisher); (ii) a structural LIPSCHITZ operator whose norm, with the Prop:transfer curvature remainder absorbed, yields a SOUND certified radius and a distribution-free conformal coverage set (AEGIS-Certify + AEGIS-Conformal); (iii) an OPERATOR-AGNOSTIC functional defined for the entire LBEN/MonDEQ/Sandwich family, not the bespoke IGNN (AEGIS-Universal). The unifying thesis - 'equilibrium sensitivity geometry with a certified attack-defense duality coupled at eps_crit' - reframes AEGIS from 'yet another sensitivity heuristic' into a fundamental object: the IFT resolvent is at once the attacker's gain, the defender's Lipschitz constant, the Fisher metric's pullback, and the detectability floor's denominator, all diverging together at the same phase transition. This single sentence broadens all four axes at once (model class via LBEN, guarantee via the sound radius/CRB/conformal triad, depth via information geometry, and reframes the diagnostic as a two-sided fundamental limit), making the 'too narrow' critique incoherent: the narrowness was an artifact of presenting one facet (the diagnostic) of a richer geometric object.

## Top pick

TOP PICK: AEGIS-Certify (Proposal 1, Tsuzuku Lipschitz-margin radius instantiated with the resolvent-derived structural Lipschitz constant). Justification on novelty x scope x feasibility. NOVELTY: the intersection 'sound, closed-form, matrix-free certified radius against adjacency perturbations from the equilibrium IFT resolvent operator norm' is verified-unoccupied; every sound structural-GNN certifier is smoothing/IBP/partition-vote and every implicit-net certifier is input-only, so the categorical claim 'first sound matrix-free structural certifier for equilibrium GNNs, emitting attack AND defense from one S_c pass' holds regardless of numeric tightness. SCOPE: it converts the paper's OWN conceded weakness (rem:certificates, conclusion limitation (i), the AGNNCert footnote, all of which state verbatim that r_v is a first-order sensitivity threshold not a certificate) into a headline theorem, killing prongs (b) 'just a Jacobian diagnostic' and (d) 'not a guarantee' at once - the highest-leverage single move because the weakness is self-identified and the fix is an honest tightening. ## The proposals

### 1. AEGIS-Certify: a sound, closed-form, matrix-free certified radius against structural attacks for equilibrium GNNs (the headline theorem)

- **Tools imported:** Tsuzuku, Sato & Sugiyama, 'Lipschitz-Margin Training', NeurIPS 2018 (arXiv:1802.04034), radius r(F,x) >= M(F,x)/(sqrt(2) L(F)), instantiated with a STRUCTURAL Lipschitz constant from the IFT resolvent operator norm. Closest sound cousins to cite as the delta: Jafarpour-Abate-Coogan, 'Robustness Certificates for Implicit Neural Networks: A Mixed Monotone Contractive Approach', L4DC 2022 (arXiv:2112.05310, input-only); Wang-Jia-Cao-Gong, 'Certified Robustness of GNNs against Adversarial Structural Perturbation', KDD 2021 (arXiv:2008.10715, smoothing-based).
- **Citations:** arXiv:1802.04034 (Tsuzuku 2018); arXiv:2112.05310 (Jafarpour L4DC 2022); arXiv:2008.10715 (Wang-Gong KDD 2021); paper-internal Thm 1a, Prop:transfer (L_J bound), Eq.(radius), rem:certificates.
- **New result:** THEOREM (AEGIS-Certify). For a contractive IGNN (A1-A3) with spectral-normalized W, every node v carries a CERTIFIED structural radius rho_v = the positive root of the per-node quadratic m_v - sqrt(2) sigma_1(S_{c,v}) ||W_{y_v}-W_c||_2 r - C_v r^2 = 0, with C_v = ||W_{y_v}-W_c||_2 (1-kappa)^{-2} L_J / 2 and L_J <= ||W||_2^2 ||X_proj||/(1-kappa), such that NO feasible symmetric edge-supported delta-Ahat with ||delta-Ahat||_F < rho_v can flip node v. rho_v is exactly the paper's existing Eq.(radius) r_v with the Prop:transfer second-order remainder ABSORBED as certified slack instead of dropped: it is the sound version of r_v. Soundness follows by integrating ||S_c(Ahat)||_op <= sigma_1(S_c)|_0 + (1-kappa)^{-2} L_J ||Ahat-Ahat_0||_F along the perturbation path (mean-value device), every ingredient of which is ALREADY PROVEN (uniform resolvent bound 1/(1-kappa) on the subcritical polytope from Thm 1a; curvature constant from Prop:transfer). One stated assumption: the curvature bound is a sup over the finitely many ReLU regions the radius-r ball crosses (standard piecewise-Lipschitz treatment).
- **Why breakthrough:** It converts the paper's single most-attacked, self-conceded weakness (rem:certificates: 'r_v is a first-order sensitivity threshold, not a probabilistic certificate... can be violated at larger magnitudes where higher-order terms dominate') into a headline theorem using only machinery the paper already proves. The intersection 'sound, closed-form, matrix-free certified radius against ADJACENCY perturbations derived from the equilibrium IFT resolvent (I-J_z)^{-1}J_A operator norm' is verified-open: every existing sound structural-GNN certifier (AGNNCert/IBP, Wang-Gong/smoothing, Bojchevski/policy-iteration) is interval-relaxed, randomized, or partition-vote; every DEQ/implicit certifier (Jafarpour 2022, Havens LBEN, Wei-Kolter) certifies input/feature L_p only, never graph structure. AEGIS uniquely emits in ONE matrix-free pass BOTH the attack (v_1 of S_c) AND a sound defense certificate against that very attack class, at N=7650 with no SDP/LP/smoothing.
- **Kills narrow scope:** GUARANTEE STRENGTH (diagnostic -> sound deterministic certificate) AND THEORETICAL DEPTH (global-Lipschitz-on-the-subcritical-polytope + curvature-absorbed radius). Directly answers prong (b) 'just a Jacobian diagnostic, incremental over certifiers' and (d) 'a diagnostic, not a guarantee' simultaneously.
- **Experiment:** PROOF: (1) state the uniform resolvent bound 1/(1-kappa) over the subcritical polytope (re-cite Thm 1a with kappa := polytope-sup of ||J_z'||_2); (2) the quadratic-root construction with C_v from Prop:transfer; (3) the ReLU-region sup as the one assumption. EMPIRICAL: replace the existing 0%-breach-at-eps=0.01 plot with a certified-accuracy-vs-budget curve on Cora/Citeseer/Pubmed/WikiCS/Amazon - report fraction of nodes with rho_v > eps and confirm 0 breaches for all ||delta||_F < rho_v across 10 seeds. Head-to-head certified accuracy and average certified radius vs AGNNCert at matched budgets. MUST report rho_v honestly even though rho_v < r_v (curvature eats the radius); frame as 'first sound matrix-free structural certifier for equilibrium GNNs', not 'tightest', until measured.
- **Effort / risk:** S / rho_v is strictly smaller than r_v, so whether it still numerically beats AGNNCert is an empirical question that MUST be measured, not asserted. A reviewer may press the global-Lipschitz claim across ReLU regions; mitigated by stating the piecewise-Lipschitz region-sup as the certificate's single explicit assumption (already how the paper handles Prop:transfer).

### 2. AEGIS-Conformal: distribution-free, finite-sample STRUCTURAL coverage certificate from the equilibrium sensitivity bound (highest-ROI guarantee, model-agnostic)

- **Tools imported:** Zargarbashi & Bojchevski, 'Robust Conformal Prediction with a Single Binary Certificate', ICLR 2025 (arXiv:2503.05239) - proves robust split-CP needs only a worst-case score-change bound over the threat set and removes the bounded-score requirement. Lineage: H-Zargarbashi/Akhondzadeh/Bojchevski, 'Conformal Prediction Sets for GNNs' (PMLR 2023-24); Jeary et al. VRCP, NeurIPS 2024; lip-rcp arXiv:2506.05434 (2025). Methodological twin to distinguish: lip-rcp/one-sample (arXiv:2506.16553) bound the score via feedforward Lipschitz products over FEATURE balls, never an implicit fixed point or edge polytope.
- **Citations:** arXiv:2503.05239 (Zargarbashi-Bojchevski ICLR 2025); H-Zargarbashi et al. PMLR 2023-24 (CP for GNNs); arXiv:2506.05434 + arXiv:2506.16553 (lip-rcp/one-sample, feature-only twins); VRCP NeurIPS 2024; paper-internal Prop:radius, Prop:transfer, Prop:explicit (S_K).
- **New result:** THEOREM (AEGIS-Conformal). Under (A1)-(A3) and exchangeability of calibration nodes, feed the analytic per-node worst-case score shift rho_v(eps) := ||(W_{y_v}-W_c) S_{c,v}||_2 * eps + (Prop:transfer curvature remainder) into the binary-certificate split-CP construction. The resulting set C_eps(v) satisfies P(y_v in C_eps(v)) >= 1 - alpha SIMULTANEOUSLY over the ENTIRE eps-ball of symmetric edge-supported adjacency perturbations - a distribution-free, finite-sample structural robustness certificate computed from an implicit model's equilibrium sensitivity rather than from randomized smoothing. The analytic S_c bound REPLACES the ~10^4-sample Monte-Carlo smoothing the construction otherwise needs, so the certificate is both sound AND cheaper. Per-edge scores v_ij additionally yield an edge-resolved certificate: coverage against ALL single/k-edge structural attacks by bounding the worst score change via max_k w_k v_k (Prop:transfer).
- **Why breakthrough:** It flips the most damaging non-physics critique (r_v 'is not a certificate, uncorrelated with the sound certifier') into the paper's strongest guarantee, and reframes AGNNCert from rival to empirical anchor (report coverage and average set-size head-to-head). The seam 'analytic equilibrium-sensitivity bound as the conformity-score Lipschitz input for STRUCTURAL robust CP on implicit GNNs' is verified-open: all prior graph-CP robustness bounds the score via smoothing/NN-verification over feature/discrete-bit perturbations; none derive it from the structural IFT resolvent over the symmetric edge polytope. Because CP is model-agnostic, the certificate applies VERBATIM to explicit GNNs via S_K (Prop:explicit), broadening past the IGNN subclass for free.
- **Kills narrow scope:** GUARANTEE STRENGTH (diagnostic -> sound distribution-free finite-sample certificate) PLUS MODEL CLASS (applies to any GNN via S_K, not just the IGNN). Answers prongs (a), (b), and (d).
- **Experiment:** Run robust split-CP with the S_c score bound at eps in {0.01,0.05,0.10} on the existing calibration/test splits (Cora/Citeseer/Pubmed/WikiCS/Amazon). Report empirical coverage (target 0.9), average set size, and head-to-head set size at matched coverage vs AGNNCert and vs smoothing-CP. ~half-page corollary + one coverage/set-size table; ~1 week compute on the existing matrix-free pipeline.
- **Effort / risk:** S / Soundness requires certifying with sigma_1(S_c)*eps + the in-paper SECOND-ORDER remainder, not the bare r_v linearization (else the certificate breaches exactly where rem:certificates says it does). Split-CP needs calibration/test EXCHANGEABILITY, which does not hold for free on a single transductive graph with message passing; cite the inductive/permutation-invariant graph-CP treatment (H-Zargarbashi 2023) and scope it as a stated condition. Both patchable, neither fatal.

### 3. AEGIS-Fisher: S_c as the pullback Fisher information metric, with a Cramer-Rao detectability floor coupled to eps_crit (the depth/duality result)

- **Tools imported:** Zhao, Fletcher, Yu et al., 'The Adversarial Attack and Detection under the Fisher Information Metric' (OSSA), AAAI 2019 (arXiv:1810.03806): the unit-Fisher-budget-optimal one-step perturbation is the leading eigenvector of the FIM. Pullback machinery: Arvanitidis et al., 'Pulling back information geometry', AISTATS 2022 (own the pullback concept - do NOT claim it). GGN=Fisher identity: cite as 'standard (Martens, JMLR 2020; Schraudolph 2002)'. CRB/van-Trees: classical. Graph-detection analogue: Hajjouz & Avksentieva, 'Detectability Thresholds for Network Attacks', arXiv:2509.10925 (2025).
- **Citations:** arXiv:1810.03806 (Zhao et al. OSSA AAAI 2019); Arvanitidis et al. AISTATS 2022 (pullback - cite, not claim); Martens JMLR 2020 + Schraudolph 2002 (GGN=Fisher); arXiv:2509.10925 (graph-detectability 2025); paper-internal Prop:attack (v_1), eps_crit.
- **New result:** THEOREM (Fisher-Cramer-Rao duality for equilibrium edge attacks). With a softmax head Fisher H = E[(d log p/dz)(d log p/dz)^T] at z*, define the edge-pullback Fisher metric G_c = S_c^T H S_c. Then (i) [attack] the local-KL-optimal symmetric edge perturbation is the top eigenvector of G_c = top right singular vector of H^{1/2} S_c; when H=I this is EXACTLY AEGIS's existing v_1 of S_c, so 'beats 50-step PGD' becomes a closed-form OSSA-theorem instance; (ii) [defense floor] no unbiased detector resolves a budget below eps_min = z_beta / sqrt(sigma_1(G_c)) = z_beta/(sigma_1(S_c) sqrt(lambda_max(H))) - an estimator-INDEPENDENT Cramer-Rao limit binding every detector; (iii) [phase coupling] sigma_1(G_c) <= lambda_max(H) ||J_A||^2/(1-kappa)^2 diverges as eps -> eps_crit, so the SAME resolvent makes an attack maximally DETECTABLE exactly where it becomes maximally damaging. MANDATORY formulation: state the damage/detectability tradeoff as a GENERALIZED eigenvalue problem with decision-boundary metric B distinct from Fisher metric H (max u^T B u s.t. u^T H u <= eps^2); with B != H this is a genuine frontier, whereas B = H collapses to a tautology.
- **Why breakthrough:** Recasts the SVD-optimal attack from an ad hoc heuristic into a canonical Riemannian-geometry theorem and attaches a two-sided damage<->detectability duality nobody has stated. Strictly richer than every cited Fisher-attack work along three orthogonal axes at once: (1) perturbs graph STRUCTURE on the edge polytope, not features; (2) the metric carries the equilibrium RESOLVENT (I-J_z)^{-1}; (3) adds an estimator-independent CRB detectability floor coupled to a contraction phase transition. Influence functions use the scalar loss-Hessian; AEGIS-Fisher uses the full predictive-distribution Fisher pulled back through an equilibrium map onto the edge polytope - a categorically richer object, killing 'incremental over influence functions'.
- **Kills narrow scope:** GUARANTEE STRENGTH (first-order threshold -> Cramer-Rao lower bound binding every detector) AND THEORETICAL DEPTH (information geometry: S_c as pullback Fisher metric, eps_crit as a Fisher-information divergence). Mild MODEL-CLASS broadening (any GNN/DEQ with a likelihood head). Answers prongs (b), (c-as-incremental), (d).
- **Experiment:** PROOF: pullback identity (one line) + OSSA quadratic-form argument (cite AAAI 2019) + CRB (textbook) + the generalized-eigenvalue frontier. NUMERICAL: compute everything in the whitened H^{1/2} S_c space (G_c is rank C-1 and ill-conditioned near criticality - never form G_c). EMPIRICAL: one ROC figure per benchmark on EXISTING data - perturb along v_1 at increasing eps, run the Fisher-score detection test, plot detection power vs eps, overlay eps_min; show sigma_1(G_c) growth as kappa->1. ~1.5 pages, one theorem + one prop + one ROC.
- **Effort / risk:** M / The headline 'cannot be simultaneously maximally damaging and minimally detectable' is VACUOUS if damage and detection use the same metric (it becomes an identity). MUST be a generalized eigenvalue problem with B != H or a top reviewer correctly calls it a tautology. Requires assuming a probabilistic softmax readout (base AEGIS is deterministic argmax); state explicitly. Novelty is 4/5 because the pullback concept and scalar CRB are off-the-shelf; originality is the structural-edge/equilibrium instantiation + resolvent-coupled detectability.

### 4. AEGIS-Universal: the S_c machinery is operator-class-agnostic over the entire monotone/Lipschitz-bounded equilibrium family (the model-class scope kill)

- **Tools imported:** Havens, Araujo, Garg, Khorrami, Hu, 'Exploiting Connections between Lipschitz Structures for Certifiably Robust DEQs', NeurIPS 2023 (OpenReview 8F3Lutda7R): the LBEN umbrella into which MonDEQ, Sandwich, SLL, AOL, CPL all reparameterize with PRESERVED Lipschitz constant. Underlying: Revay-Wang-Manchester, LBEN (arXiv:2010.01732, already cited revay2020lipschitz); Winston-Kolter, Monotone Operator Equilibrium Networks, NeurIPS 2020 (cited winston2020monotone).
- **Citations:** OpenReview 8F3Lutda7R (Havens et al. NeurIPS 2023); arXiv:2010.01732 (Revay LBEN); Winston-Kolter NeurIPS 2020; arXiv:2302.08051 (AdvImmune, distinguish defense mechanism); paper-internal Prop:attack, Prop:transfer, Prop:radius, Thm 1, Obs:eta_bound.
- **New result:** THEOREM (operator-agnostic AEGIS). Every AEGIS diagnostic depends ONLY on (i) a fixed-point operator F with a contraction/monotonicity modulus and (ii) its Jacobian pair (J_z, J_A) via S = (I-J_z)^{-1}J_A. Since MonDEQ, Sandwich, SLL, AOL, CPL are all instances of one equilibrium map z* = solve(z = sigma(Wz+Ux+b)) with a structured Lipschitz prescription, the constrained S_c, the v_1 attack (Prop:attack), the per-edge transfer backbone d_k ~ w_k v_k (Prop:transfer), the per-node radius r_v (Prop:radius), and eps_crit are well-defined for the WHOLE family, with kappa replaced by the structure's prescribed modulus (for MonDEQ with strong-monotonicity m and Lipschitz L the Peaceman-Rachford factor rho_PR = sqrt(1 - 4 alpha m/(1+alpha L)^2)). COROLLARY (trainable-eps_crit defense): because LBEN supports structure-preserving fine-tuning, eps_crit becomes a TRAINABLE objective - regularize to push the observed 2-4x spectral-radius margin to a target, so AEGIS prescribes a certified-robust retraining target rather than only diagnosing.
- **Why breakthrough:** Directly dissolves the headline 'tied to the IGNN/equilibrium subclass' critique by proving the subclass is, up to reparameterization, the canonical modern implicit-model family. The cross-class structural-sensitivity result is verified-open: Havens'23 supplies the unification as a TOOL but only for image classification under L2 input perturbations - never graph, never structural/edge sensitivity, never an attack direction or transfer backbone. The trainable-eps_crit hook turns the diagnostic into a defense, distinct in mechanism from edge-immunization (AdvImmune) and dynamic-margin training.
- **Kills narrow scope:** MODEL CLASS (ReLU-IGNN -> entire MonDEQ/Sandwich/SLL/AOL/CPL equilibrium family). The single most direct answer to prong (a). Secondary GUARANTEE strengthening via the trainable-eps_crit defense corollary.
- **Experiment:** PROOF: restate Thm 1 / Prop:attack / Prop:radius with kappa -> structure modulus (re-derivation), PLUS a NEW lemma for how the modulus degrades under delta-Ahat for structured W (the rho_PR/||W|| denominator must be DERIVED, not asserted). EMPIRICAL: train ONE non-IGNN instance (MonDEQ-GNN or Sandwich-GNN) on existing datasets, rerun the v_1 attack + transfer Kendall-tau, show the same results hold with eps_crit read from the design parameter. Fits the existing 39-cell harness as one extra model x a few datasets.
- **Effort / risk:** M / Pair as the BREADTH axis only, not the depth driver - novelty is recombination (LBEN, MonDEQ-GNN, trainable-margin exist separately). Three math steps need real work, not restatement: (1) the eps_crit denominator must be re-derived for structured W; (2) 'removes A3 / inherited exactly from design certificate' must be softened to a looser gamma->||J_z|| bound (LBEN bounds input-output gamma, not ||dF/dz|| at z*); (3) the Kronecker eta Observation does not transplant to non-Kronecker Sandwich/SLL operators and must be re-proved or scoped out.

### 5. AEGIS-Tight: non-Euclidean contraction + mixed-monotone reachability for a SOUND per-edge structural radius (depth via a second, independent certificate route)

- **Tools imported:** Jafarpour, Davydov, Proskurnikov, Bullo, 'Robust Implicit Networks via Non-Euclidean Contractions' (NEMON), NeurIPS 2021 (arXiv:2106.03194); Jafarpour-Abate-Davydov-Bullo-Coogan, L4DC 2022 (arXiv:2112.05310) mixed-monotone embedded-network certificate; Davydov-Jafarpour-Bullo, 'Non-Euclidean Contraction Theory', IEEE TAC 67(12), 2022.
- **Citations:** arXiv:2106.03194 (NEMON NeurIPS 2021); arXiv:2112.05310 (Jafarpour L4DC 2022, embedded network); IEEE TAC 67(12) 2022 (Davydov non-Euclidean contraction); paper-internal Obs:eta_bound (rescope target), Prop:radius.
- **New result:** RESCOPED THEOREM (sound structural reachable-set radius). Build the mixed-monotone embedded network: an augmented 2Nd map that over-approximates z*(A) under an L_infinity box of symmetric edge-supported perturbations in TWO forward passes, yielding a SOUND lower bound on the per-node/per-edge radius - the FIRST structural (edge) certifier via contraction, upgrading r_v from a first-order threshold to a sound certified L_infinity edge-box radius. The non-Euclidean machinery additionally supplies a transient-amplification reading of (I-J_z)^{-1} as the steady-state transfer operator with induced gain 1/(1-mu_{p,Q}) in a weighted norm.
- **Why breakthrough:** Provides a SECOND, independent sound-certificate route (interval/reachability) that brackets the AEGIS-Certify first-order claim from a different direction, and fills a real gap: no prior work puts a contraction certificate on STRUCTURAL/edge sensitivity rather than feature sensitivity (NEMON/Jafarpour certify input perturbations only; Wang-Gong/Bojchevski are smoothing/policy-iteration, not contraction). De-risks the headline certificate by giving a non-linearized enclosure for the regime where AEGIS-Certify's curvature slack is loose.
- **Kills narrow scope:** GUARANTEE STRENGTH (diagnostic -> sound reachable-set certificate over a structured polytope), answering prong (d) from a second independent mechanism. The embedded-network is activation/norm-agnostic (secondary model-class breadth).
- **Experiment:** Implement the 2Nd augmented map; certify the L_infinity edge-box radius in two forward passes; plot certified-radius-vs-smoothing on the existing citation + Amazon graphs. One theorem + one figure, ~1 page.
- **Effort / risk:** M / CRITICAL - SCOPE HARD to the embedding-based radius ONLY. The proposal's two flagship selling points are UNSOUND as written and must be CUT: (1) the cheap un-weighted matrix measure mu_infty does NOT beat kappa (verified mu_infty=1.05-1.51 > kappa=||J_z||_2=0.60; it gives a WORSE critical budget); (2) the 'eta-slack is removable / phase boundary becomes tight' claim only reproduces the paper's existing all-active Observation and FAILS on the realistic ReLU-masked operator where the slack lives. Also mu_infty needs absolute row sums that JVP/VJP cannot produce in O(1). Keep ONLY the embedded-network certificate (genuinely sound and cheap); demote weighted-norm tightness to a remark scoped to the all-active case.

## Still missing / under-explored (highest-upside untaken axes)

STILL MISSING / UNDER-EXPLORED - three high-upside directions no surviving candidate captures. (1) POISONING / TRAINING-TIME via a NESTED IFT (cut from survivors but the deepest untaken axis): differentiate z*(A) AND the trained weights W*(A) jointly through structure to get a structural POISONING sensitivity S_poison = composition of the equilibrium resolvent with the training-influence resolvent. This broadens the THREAT MODEL (evasion -> poisoning, test-time -> training-time) and is the cleanest kill of 'incremental over influence functions': AEGIS would be the first to COMPOSE the two resolvents, strictly more than influence functions (training-only, no equilibrium) or AEGIS-v1 (equilibrium-only, no training). Anchor: NTK-MILP GNN poisoning certificate + bilevel IFT. Higher-effort (L) but the one axis that adds a genuinely new theorem-class rather than a guarantee-upgrade of existing machinery. (2) SPECTRAL-GEOMETRY GROUNDING of the transfer backbone via effective-resistance/commute-time bounds on the message-passing Jacobian: this would PROVE the empirical tau~0.99 per-edge ranking from pure graph geometry (a conservation-law lower bound), converting the paper's most striking empirical result from correlation into theory and killing 'incremental' from the spectral-graph-theory side, which no certificate/Fisher candidate touches. (3) DOMAIN broadening beyond citation graphs with a TRANSFER GUARANTEE: none of the survivors decisively answers prong (c). A graphon-limit argument (S_c converges to a graphon sensitivity operator under cut-distance, so the vulnerability backbone is a property of the limit, not the sampled graph) would make the diagnostics provably SIZE- and DOMAIN-transferable, addressing 'only citation graphs' with a convergence-rate guarantee rather than just more datasets. STRONGEST COMPLETE PACKAGE: TOP PICK (deterministic certificate) + RUNNER-UP (conformal/model-class) for the guarantee axis, AEGIS-Universal for model class, AEGIS-Fisher for depth/duality, PLUS the nested-IFT poisoning theorem (1) to claim the threat-model axis - that set broadens all four axes with two genuinely new theorem-classes (poisoning, Fisher-CRB) plus two guarantee-upgrades (deterministic + conformal certificates).
