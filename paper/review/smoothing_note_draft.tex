% --- (A) one-liner for sec:conformal (drop in after the gate discussion) ---
Against randomized smoothing on the \emph{same} $\varepsilon$-ball, \AEGIS-Conformal is non-vacuous where smoothing degenerates to the full label set, at ${\sim}10^4\times$ lower wall-clock (zero-sample vs $M$ reconvergence passes; \cref{app:smoothing}).

% --- (C) appendix prose ---
On the same Frobenius $\varepsilon$-ball (frob), the matched smoothing $\sigma$ is so small that the smoothed classifier is unchanged and abstains to the full $7$-label set (coverage $1.00$, set $7.00$, zero certified nodes, with \emph{zero} variance across seeds): randomized smoothing is vacuous at the perturbation scale \AEGIS targets. Only on a strictly larger per-coordinate ball (per\_edge, $\sigma{=}\varepsilon$) does smoothing certify ($0.77$--$0.96$ of nodes), and then at $\mathbf{23{,}000}$--$\mathbf{57{,}000}\times$ the wall-clock of the zero-sample $S_c$ bound. Even on the matched ball where the comparison is fairest, the analytic certificate is non-vacuous and $\mathbf{11{,}700}\times$ ($\varepsilon{=}0.01$) to $\mathbf{16{,}700}\times$ ($\varepsilon{=}0.05$) faster. The comparison favors smoothing twice over---its larger ball and its best matching---yet $S_c$ dominates wall-clock by four orders of magnitude.
