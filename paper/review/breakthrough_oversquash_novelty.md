## 1. Closest prior work (8 papers) — what they do / why it is different

**Over-squashing (all finite-depth message passing):**

1. **Alon & Yahav 2021, "On the Bottleneck of GNNs" (arXiv:2006.05205, ICLR'21).** *Does:* names over-squashing — exponentially growing receptive-field information crushed into fixed-size vectors over long paths; proposes fully-adjacent last layer. *Different:* purely a finite-depth bottleneck argument, no Jacobian, no operator, no fixed point.

2. **Topping, Di Giovanni, Chamberlain, Dong, Bronstein 2021/22, "…via curvature" (arXiv:2111.14522, ICLR'22).** *Does:* first *precise* over-squashing description; introduces balanced-Forman edge curvature; negatively curved edges cause it; curvature-based rewiring. *Different:* local edge-geometry mechanism for **finite-depth** MPNNs; no equilibrium, no robustness, no margin↔reach trade-off.

3. **Di Giovanni, Giusti, Barbero, Luise, Lió, Bronstein 2023, "…Impact of Width, Depth, and Topology" (arXiv:2302.02941, ICML'23).** *Does:* THE Jacobian-sensitivity formalism `‖∂h_u^{(K)}/∂x_v‖ ≤ (∏_l ‖W^{(l)}‖)(Â^K)_{uv}`; proves width helps (at a global-sensitivity cost), **depth does NOT** (vanishing gradients dominate), topology (commute time) dominates. *Different — this is the single most-overlapping technical anchor:* it is explicitly **fixed-K, finite-depth**, and its headline is *depth cannot help*. Our thesis is the **K→∞ equilibrium** object the very same authors stopped short of: the resolvent sum `Σ_{k≥d(u,v)} (Â^k)_{uv} W^k`, whose rate is the **spectral radius ρ(W)**, not the **norm-product ∏_l‖W^{(l)}‖** (which has no K→∞ limit for untied weights). Their "depth doesn't help" is a statement about *unrolled finite networks with vanishing gradients*; the implicit model sidesteps it because reach is set by ρ(W) through the resolvent, decoupled from (unbounded) iteration count. Position as continuation, not contradiction.

4. **Di Giovanni, Rusch, Bronstein, Deac, Lackenby, Mishra, Veličković 2023, "How does over-squashing affect the power of GNNs?" (arXiv:2306.03589, TMLR'24).** *Does:* over-squashing as a *capacity* limit on learnable pairwise-interaction order; impossibility results tied to commute time. *Different:* expressivity/impossibility framing for MPNNs of given capacity; still no equilibrium operator, no robustness, no computable margin↔reach control curve.

5. **Black, Wan, Nayyeri, Wang 2023, "…through the Lens of Effective Resistance" (arXiv:2302.06835, ICML'23).** *Does:* bounds *total* over-squashing by **total effective resistance**; resistance-minimizing rewiring. *Different:* a **graph-only**, model-agnostic spectral quantity of the *input* graph; no model Jacobian, no equilibrium, no robustness. (Note: the team's internal R2.4 proposal *connects our resolvent series to a Green's-function/effective-resistance reading* — that connection is novel here precisely because Black grounds resistance in the bare graph, not in `(I−J_z)⁻¹`.)

6. **Nguyen, Nong, Nguyen, Ho, Osher, Nguyen 2022/23, "Revisiting Over-smoothing and Over-squashing via Ollivier-Ricci Curvature" (arXiv:2211.15779, ICML'23).** *Does:* unifies over-smoothing (positive curvature) and over-squashing (negative curvature) at a *local* scale; Batch-Ollivier-Ricci-Flow rewiring. *Different:* a *two-phenomenon* unification via **curvature**, finite-depth, no robustness, no operator/resolvent, no convergence leg.

7. **Gravina, Bacciu, Gallicchio 2022/23, "Anti-Symmetric DGN" (arXiv:2210.09789, ICLR'23).** *Does:* ODE-derived **non-dissipative** DGN; antisymmetric weights ⇒ no gradient vanish/explode ⇒ long-range preserved over dozens of layers. *Different:* a *constructive architecture* whose conserved dynamics avoid over-squashing; no resolvent characterization, no margin↔reach *trade-off* statement, no robustness object. Closest in *spirit* (stability ⇄ long-range), but mechanism and claims differ.

8. **Arroyo, Gravina, Gutteridge, Barbero, Gallicchio, Dong, Bronstein, Vandergheynst 2025, "On Vanishing Gradients, Over-Smoothing, and Over-Squashing in GNNs" (arXiv:2502.10818, v2 Oct 2025).** *Does:* a **unified view of over-smoothing + over-squashing + vanishing gradients through ONE lens (vanishing gradients), using linear control theory** on a **state-space / recurrent** GNN. *Different — this is the most threatening unification:* it unifies a *different triple* (the third leg is **vanishing gradients**, not **adversarial robustness**) via the **finite-depth/recurrent gradient** (BPTT Jacobian products), **not** the **equilibrium resolvent (I−J_z)⁻¹ of an implicit fixed-point model**, and it contains **no adversarial-robustness leg and no spectral-margin↔reach Pareto curve / robustness-cost certificate**. Our unification swaps "vanishing gradients" for "adversarial/structural robustness," moves from recurrent-unrolled to **infinite-depth equilibrium**, and adds the **opposing-sign control** that turns one knob into a *trade*, not a co-monotone bundle.

**Implicit / equilibrium GNNs (the "infinite-depth" family the thesis lives in):**

9. **Gu, Chang, Zhu, Sojoudi, El Ghaoui 2020, "Implicit Graph Neural Networks / IGNN" (arXiv:2009.06211, NeurIPS'20).** *Does:* fixed-point `z*=F(z*,A)`; **Perron-Frobenius** well-posedness; implicit-diff training; *claims long-range capture*. *Different:* well-posedness via PF spectral *threshold*, and long-range is an *empirical* claim — there is **no resolvent off-diagonal-decay over-squashing theorem, no margin↔reach trade-off, no robustness object**. This is the architecture our thesis analyzes; the analysis is the new part.

10. **Liu, Kawaguchi, Hooi, Wang, Xiao 2022, "EIGNN: Efficient Infinite-Depth GNN" (arXiv:2202.10720, NeurIPS'21).** *Does:* infinite-depth GNN with **closed-form** solution (eigendecomposition); long-range capture; *and explicitly "more robust against noise and adversarial perturbations."* *Different — the most threatening implicit-GNN paper:* it is the one prior work that *empirically co-locates* long-range + adversarial robustness in an infinite-depth GNN. But it is a **linear** closed-form model, it **does not characterize over-squashing via the resolvent's off-diagonal decay**, it gives **no spectral-margin↔reach trade-off**, and crucially **no opposing-control statement** (it markets robustness *and* range as jointly improved — the *opposite* of our falsifiable sign-conflict claim). Our contribution is to show, on the contrary, that under spectral regularization robustness and reach **trade off** through the same operator.

11. **Park, Choo, Park 2021/22, "Convergent Graph Solvers (CGS)" (arXiv:2106.01680).** *Does:* learns **contractive** linear iterative maps, Banach fixed point ⇒ guaranteed convergence; implicit-diff gradients. *Different:* contractivity is used only for **convergence/well-posedness**; no over-squashing, no robustness, no trade-off — exactly the "convergence leg" our thesis unifies *with the other two*.

12. **Winston & Kolter 2020, "Monotone Operator Equilibrium Networks (monDEQ)" (arXiv:2006.08591, NeurIPS'20).** *Does:* DEQ parameterized so the operator is **monotone** ⇒ unique equilibrium + stable convergent solvers. *Different:* foundational *stability/uniqueness* lineage for implicit depth; no graph distance / over-squashing, no robustness-vs-reach trade-off. (General DEQ: Bai, Kolter, Koltun 2019.)

13. **Chen, Wang, Wang, Yang, Lin 2022, "Graph Implicit Nonlinear Diffusion (GIND)" (arXiv:2206.14418, ICML'22).** *Does:* implicit infinite-hop nonlinear diffusion; equilibrium = minimizer of a **convex energy**; long-range + anti-over-smoothing. *Different:* energy/optimization characterization of the equilibrium; **no resolvent-decay over-squashing law, no margin↔reach trade-off, no robustness unification**.

## 2. The precise novel delta of the thesis

Decomposed leg-by-leg, with an honest "new vs. known" tag:

- **(D1) Over-squashing = off-diagonal decay of the equilibrium resolvent — [PARTIALLY NEW].** The exact identity `∂z*_u/∂x_v = [ (I−J_z)⁻¹ ]_{(u,v)} · U`, with support law `[R]_{(u,v)} = Σ_{k≥d(u,v)} (Â^k)_{uv} W^k`, is the **infinite-depth** statement of over-squashing. *New part:* it is the K→∞ resolvent form for an **implicit** model, with the deterministic envelope κ^{d(u,v)} (ReLU-robust) and the **sharp asymptotic rate (c_G·ρ(W))^{d(u,v)} — a spectral radius, not a norm product**. *Known part:* it is recognizably the fixed-point limit of Di Giovanni et al.'s `(Â^K)_{uv}` bound; alone it is incremental. **The novelty is the operator (resolvent) and the rate (ρ(W)), not the existence of distance-decay.**

- **(D2) Spectral-margin (1−κ) ↔ long-range-reach trade-off — [NEW for implicit GNNs].** Effective range `L_eff = Θ(1/(1−κ))` diverges as the contraction margin closes, while worst-case structural sensitivity `σ_1(S) = Θ(1/(1−ρ(J_z)))` *also* diverges at the same pole. No prior implicit-GNN work (IGNN/EIGNN/CGS/GIND/monDEQ) states a margin↔reach law; A-DGN gives stability⇄range qualitatively but not as a *computable Pareto curve* in a contraction margin. The arXiv conjunctive searches for "implicit GNN long-range spectral contraction" and "equilibrium network stability expressivity trade-off spectral radius" returned **zero results**.

- **(D3) Unification of over-squashing + adversarial robustness + convergence in ONE operator — [NEW, and the headline].** The *non-tautological* content is the **opposing-control / sign-conflict** structure: tightening ‖W‖ (spectral regularization) *improves* robustness (smaller pole σ_1(S_c)) and convergence (Neumann depth `K=O(log(1/tol)/(1−κ))`) **but worsens** reach (steeper off-diagonal decay). Same matrix `(I−J_z)⁻¹`, **two different functionals** (top singular value / near-1 pole vs. entrywise decay exponent), **opposite response to one knob** ⇒ a falsifiable Pareto prediction (the eq.-6 frontier). No external work ties *adversarial/structural robustness* to over-squashing through one operator; Arroyo'25 unifies a different triple (vanishing gradients) without robustness and without the equilibrium resolvent; EIGNN co-locates robustness+range empirically but claims them *co-improving*, the opposite of the sign conflict.

- **(D4) A matrix-free, infinite-depth over-squashing meter — [NEW capability].** AEGIS's shipped `Neumann Σ_k J_z^k b + randomized SVD` (validated to N=7650) *already computes resolvent-vector products* for structural sensitivity; repurposing it yields a per-node *converged* long-range-capacity profile that finite-depth theory cannot produce, plus a *principled* hyperparameter (set ‖W‖_2 to target L_eff, read off the robustness cost from the same S_c query).

**One-line delta:** *the infinite-depth resolvent characterization (D1) + the computable margin↔reach trade-off (D2) are the technical novelty; the opposing-control robustness/over-squashing duality (D3) is the breakthrough-grade claim; together they are unoccupied in the literature.*

## 3. Honest verdict

**PARTIALLY-COVERED.** Three independent groups already crowd the *neighbourhood*: Di Giovanni et al. own the **finite-depth Jacobian-sensitivity** mechanism (D1's known half); Arroyo et al. 2025 own a **one-lens unification of three GNN pathologies** (a different triple, finite-depth, no robustness, no equilibrium resolvent); EIGNN owns **infinite-depth + empirical robustness + long-range** (but co-improving, linear, no resolvent-decay theorem). So a reviewer *will* see prior art for each individual ingredient.

**What survives as genuinely new** is the **conjunction no one has assembled**: over-squashing as the *equilibrium resolvent's off-diagonal decay* (implicit/infinite-depth, ρ(W)-rate) **and** the *same resolvent's near-1 pole* as the robustness object, with a **falsifiable opposing-sign control law** (the margin↔reach/robustness Pareto), computable matrix-free at scale. That triangulation — over-squashing ⇄ adversarial robustness ⇄ convergence through one *implicit-GNN* resolvent, with sign conflict — is not in any surveyed work.

**Narrowest defensibly-new claim (recommend as headline):**
> *In contractive implicit GNNs, the equilibrium resolvent `(I−J_z)⁻¹` controls both over-squashing and structural (adversarial) robustness through two distinct spectral functionals — its off-diagonal decay rate (reach) and its near-1 pole (worst-case sensitivity) — which respond with opposite sign to the spectral-norm knob ‖W‖, yielding a computable robustness/long-range Pareto frontier with no finite-depth analogue.*

Do **not** headline "the resolvent governs over-squashing" alone (it reads as the K→∞ restatement of Di Giovanni's `Â^K` bound), nor "everything depends on `(I−J_z)⁻¹`" (tautology — *every* implicit-model quantity is a resolvent functional; the substance is the *two-functional, opposing-sign* structure). This matches the team's own internal self-assessment in `breakthrough_oversquash_theory.md` §5, now corroborated against the external record.

## 4. Strongest related-work positioning sentence (drop-in)

> *Whereas prior over-squashing theory bounds the **finite-depth** node-pair Jacobian by a single adjacency power `(Â^K)_{uv}` and concludes that depth cannot extend reach (Topping et al., 2022; Di Giovanni et al., 2023), and recent unifications fold over-squashing together with over-smoothing and vanishing gradients through the **recurrent** gradient (Nguyen et al., 2023; Arroyo et al., 2025), we show that for **implicit, infinite-depth** GNNs the relevant object is instead the **equilibrium resolvent** `(I−J_z)⁻¹ = Σ_k Â^k ⊗ W^k`: over-squashing is its off-diagonal decay at the spectral-radius rate ρ(W) (with no finite-depth analogue, since the norm product `∏_l‖W^{(l)}‖` does not converge as `K→∞`), while its near-1 pole simultaneously governs adversarial/structural sensitivity — so the single contraction-margin knob `1−κ` trades long-range reach against robustness and convergence speed along one computable frontier, unifying with the AEGIS auditing operator three phenomena that finite-depth and recurrent analyses treat separately.*
